2.5.326 — page-hook → content.js event queue with INITIALIZED handshake
- page-hook.js now holds intercepted Sim Companies network events (API_DATA, API_SEEN, stock mutations, realm switches, market cache, game state, etc.) in an ordered queue (max 500, oldest dropped and counted) until content.js posts 'INITIALIZED'. It then flushes the queue in order, replies 'INITIALIZED_ACK' {first, flushed, dropped, queuedTotal}, and posts later events directly.
- Never queued: HOOK_READY and replies to content.js requests (API_RESULT, diagnostics, capture ACKs, sync-realm replies, resource images), since content.js is listening when it asks.
- Fallback: if no handshake arrives within 15 s the queue is flushed anyway and 'BRIDGE_QUEUE_FLUSHED' is posted, so behaviour is never worse than before.
- content.js posts INITIALIZED at the end of the file (after every listener is attached) and again on HOOK_READY until acknowledged (covers the hook starting after content.js). Repeated INITIALIZED is harmless.
- Why: window.postMessage is fire-and-forget; events posted before content.js attached its listener were lost. Verified in Chromium: with 2.5.325 a late listener received nothing; with 2.5.326 it received the early response. Added tests/bridge-queue-handshake.test.js (runs the real queue code).
